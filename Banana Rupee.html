WELCOME TO NAYI DISHA

How to play Banana Rupee

Explore the town, make money choices, and see how your decisions affect your savings and neighbours.

1
Explore the mapTap a labelled place to visit it, a neighbour to talk, or a floating coin to collect ₹50.
2
Choose what to doRead each situation and pick an option. Your wallet, savings, happiness, and XP can change.
3
Build up the townUse the town projects panel to add street lanterns or a community garden.
4
Keep playingEnd day to advance the story and earn ₹100. Daylight lasts 10 minutes; night lasts 5 minutes.
Need a hand? Tap the floating Ask Chai button at the lower-right for money explanations, weather controls, and the Town updates switch. Chai can make small automatic game events about every three minutes while updates are enabled. Your learning and gameplay stats are at the bottom of the page.
