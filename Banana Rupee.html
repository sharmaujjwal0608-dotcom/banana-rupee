WELCOME TO NAYI DISHA

How to play Banana Rupee

Explore the town, make money choices, and see how your decisions affect your savings and neighbours.

1
Explore the mapTap or click an open spot to walk, drag to turn the town, and scroll to zoom. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to move. Walk close to a place or neighbour, then press E to open choices. Collect a nearby banana with E for ₹50.
2
Choose what to doRead each situation and pick an option. Your wallet, savings, happiness, and XP can change.
3
Build up the townBuild lanterns, a garden, a playground, or safer crossings. Projects cost rupees and raise town happiness.
4
Keep playingEarn ₹100 at the end of each day, take paid shifts in town, and pick fresh bananas. Spend on snacks, fares, repairs, and projects.
Need a hand? Tap the floating Ask Tea button at the lower-right for money explanations and weather controls. Open the three-line menu to view the Town dashboard, pause Tea’s town updates, check debt, or open your gameplay stats. Tea can make small automatic game events about every three minutes while updates are enabled.
