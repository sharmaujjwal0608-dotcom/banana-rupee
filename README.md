# Banana Rupee

**Empowering Indian Teenagers**

A single-file browser money adventure for teenagers. Explore a detailed street map of Nayi Disha, make everyday money choices, collect coins, build community projects, and see how decisions affect your savings and neighbours.

## Play

Open `Banana Rupee.html` in a modern browser. The How to play guide opens when the game starts; reopen it from the three-line menu beside the logo.
The layout adapts to phones and tablets, including touch-friendly controls and graphs that fit narrow screens. Learning and gameplay stats open in a popup from the three-line menu.

- Click the player name to change it. A fresh or reset game starts as **Cracklers**; names and progress save in this browser.
- Choose **Reset game** from the three-line menu to clear saved progress after confirmation. It returns to Day 0, sunny weather, starting coins, Level 1, and the name Cracklers.
- Tap a labelled building or a named person on the detailed town map. Follow the roads between the school, clinic, library, bank, market, cafe, park, and bus stop. Each location and neighbour has its own conversation and choices.
- Tap a floating coin to collect ₹50. Build street lanterns or a community garden from the town panel.
- Choices earn XP. Every five choices advances your level.
- Open the three-line menu beside the logo for **How to play**, **End day**, **Learning & gameplay stats**, and **Reset game**. The stats option opens the graphs in a popup. End day advances the story and gives ₹100 for helping at the shop.
- The Learning & gameplay stats popup shows concept practice and wallet/savings/town-happiness changes, alongside weather, level, and gameplay totals. The graph displays the latest 24 events; up to 60 are saved in this browser.
- Daylight lasts 10 real-time minutes and night lasts 5 minutes, repeating. Weather is sunny by day; at night only cloudy or rainy conditions are used. Auto mode switches conditions every two minutes. Chai’s Town updates are on by default and add a small market, savings-practice, or community event about every three minutes; pause or resume them in the Chai chat. These are game rewards, not real interest rates.
- Tap the fixed **Ask Chai** button in the lower-right corner to chat about basic money topics or control the weather. It stays in place while you scroll. Use the Sunny, Cloudy, or Rainy buttons, say “make it rainy”, or choose **Auto** to let Chai rotate weather. The Town updates control lets Chai automatically update wallet, savings, or happiness with small game events.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

Upload `Banana Rupee.html` (and optionally this README and `banana-rupee-project.md`) to a GitHub repository. Open **Settings → Pages**, select **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.

Game data stays in this browser and is not sent to a server. Chai is a built-in offline helper, not a cloud AI service.
