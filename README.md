# Banana Play

## Nayi Disha · Money Adventure

Banana Play is a browser-based financial literacy game set in the fictional town of Nayi Disha. Explore the neighbourhood, meet residents, take paid work, help neighbours, and make choices about spending, saving, borrowing, and everyday costs.

## Play

Open [Banana Rupee.html](Banana%20Rupee.html) in a modern browser, or publish the repository with GitHub Pages. Enter a player name and start with ₹2,500.

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. Press **E** near a person or place to interact. You can also select locations on the map.
- Explore jobs, the market, bank, clinic, library, park, and other places around town.
- Track your wallet, savings, debt, energy, and five-day plan. Use the ledger to review money activity and **End day** to advance the story.
- After the five-day challenge, review your money story and continue exploring.
- Choose **Reset** in the top bar to clear all saved game progress and return to the start. The game asks for confirmation first.

Your progress is saved in this browser on this device. Resetting clears the saved game, including money, savings, debt, items, day, and activity history.
